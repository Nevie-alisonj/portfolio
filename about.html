About Me

I am a BSc Immunology student at the Malawi University of Science and Technology (MUST), with practical experience in clinical laboratory settings and a growing interest in scientific innovation. As President of the MUST Society of Immunology, I lead a community of 86 members, promoting academic engagement, research and professional development among students.

My interest in infectious disease diagnostics grew through my hospital laboratory attachment, where I gained exposure to routine diagnostic procedures and saw the importance of reliable testing in patient care. This experience inspired me to explore innovative diagnostic solutions, including PlasmoID, a proposed rapid diagnostic test designed to detect five malaria parasite species in a single sample. I am motivated by the opportunity to contribute to accessible, accurate, and affordable diagnostics for communities facing infectious disease challenges.

I am currently developing my skills in innovation, intellectual property and R programming and data analysis, while exploring ideas that address real-world health and environmental challenges. My long-term goal is to pursue a PhD specializing in immunology and infectious disease diagnostics, combining laboratory science, research and innovation to help develop diagnostic solutions that improve health outcomes, particularly in resource-limited settings.
